sphere, "shape bay" containing an elongated ellipse, "cool bay" containing an arc, and
"harvest bay" containing a rectangular ingot. Dashed curved arrows run between them around
the ring showing the process sequence. An arrow enters from the upper left labeled "feedstock
in: refinery · mass driver · scrap," and an arrow exits lower right labeled "→ great

containerless bay in cross-section: horizontal "levitation field coils" above and below, a

dimension arrow to the gap labeled "touches nothing," captioned "surface tension pulls it
