# Gravitational Lens Telescope Fleet "Cyclops" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "GL-1 Cyclops," a spacecraft that uses the
Sun as a gravitational lens to image exoplanets, drawn as a vintage cyanotype
schematic: deep prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan linework, faint
drafting grid. Horizontal optical-path schematic reading left to right: at far
left, a small exoplanet labeled "target world" emitting light rays; the rays
travel right toward a stylized Sun with rays in the center-left; the light bends
around the Sun (cyan curved rays labeled "light bent by gravity") and converges
onto a dashed focal line to the right labeled "focus begins ~550 AU"; at the right
end of the focal line sits a small telescope spacecraft with a coronagraph disc,
tube optics, and two solar panels, labeled "Cyclops telescope," with small cyan
scan-motion arrows. Inset panel lower-right showing the Sun with three separate
focal lines radiating to three small telescope icons, captioned "one per target,
each line aims at one star system." Magnified detail circle showing an Einstein
ring: a blocked black solar disc in the center surrounded by a bright cyan ring
labeled "Einstein ring = lensed planet," captioned "reconstruct map from ring
scan." Numbered callout circles with leader lines, a parts list panel, and an
engineering title block reading "GL-1 CYCLOPS — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced
drafting typography, flat orthographic projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (the pathfinder telescope spacecraft en route to the focus)

```
Photorealistic render of a compact deep-space telescope spacecraft cruising through
the outer solar system, far from the Sun: a slender cylindrical telescope body
about the size of a car with a black coronagraph disc on a boom extended in front
of its aperture, wrapped in gold and black thermal insulation, with a large
high-gain dish antenna aimed back toward a tiny distant Sun, compact solar panels,
and a radioisotope power unit with fins. In the background, the Sun appears as an
intensely bright but small star amid a dense black starfield, emphasizing enormous
distance. Fine engineering detail: micro-thrusters for fine positioning, star
trackers, a serial label "SGL PATHFINDER 1," radiator panels, cabling. The scene
is cold, dark, and vast. Shot from a three-quarter angle, hard pinpoint sunlight
from behind, deep black space with sharp stars, hyper-detailed, aerospace
documentary photography style.
```
