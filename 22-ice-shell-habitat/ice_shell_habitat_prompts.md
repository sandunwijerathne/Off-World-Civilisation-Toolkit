# Ice-Shell Habitat "Cistern" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "IS-1 Cistern," a habitat whose radiation
shield is also its water reserve, drawn as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep
prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan linework, faint drafting grid. Main
view is a cross-section of nested spheres: a large outer circle and a slightly
smaller circle inside it, with the band between them filled with fine diagonal
hatching labeled "ice shell (frozen, metres thick) = radiation shield + water
reserve." Inside that, a third circle is the "inner pressure hull, warm living
volume," drawn with a couple of horizontal deck lines and two tiny stick-figure
crew. A small gap between hull and ice is labeled "insulation gap." From the upper
right, white arrows labeled "GCR / solar particles" strike the ice shell and stop
in small cyan absorption bursts labeled "absorbed in hydrogen-rich water." A small
circle at the top of the shell is a "heated tap-off (melt)" with an arrow drawing
water inward. Small radiating lines on the outer lower surface are labeled "outer
face radiates → stays frozen." Inset panel top-right titled "the wall is a tank":
a hatched ice-shell block with an arrow out labeled "melt → drink / crops /
electrolysis (O2 + fuel)" and an arrow back in labeled "reclaimed water →
refreeze," plus "top up from local ice (moon / asteroid)." Magnified detail circle
showing a layered shield stack from top to bottom — space, a thick hatched ice
band labeled "ice (H-rich)," a thin "hull" layer, and "crew (warm, safe)" — with a
side arrow labeled "dose drops." A dimension line labeled "shell thickness = shield
depth = reserve volume." Numbered callout circles, a parts list panel, and a title
block reading "IS-1 CISTERN — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography,
flat orthographic projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: water-walled storm shelter module)

```
Photorealistic cutaway render of a near-future spacecraft storm-shelter module: a
cylindrical crew compartment whose walls are transparent-faced water tanks, so the
thick layer of water surrounding the small inner living space is clearly visible,
faintly blue, with a few bubbles and frost forming on the cold outer skin. Inside
the snug shirt-sleeve interior, one astronaut in a soft flight suit floats reading a
tablet, sleep restraints and a laptop and water dispenser visible, warm interior
lighting. The water jacket is plumbed with visible pipes, valves, and a small
heater tap-off point where liquid water is drawn. Fine engineering detail: tank
baffles, gold thermal foil on the exterior, a printed label "STORM SHELTER / WATER
WALL," handrails, cabling. Through a small window, black space and the edge of a
larger station structure. Shot as a clean technical cutaway, cool exterior light
and warm interior light, hyper-detailed, aerospace documentary photography style,
realistic and grounded.
```
