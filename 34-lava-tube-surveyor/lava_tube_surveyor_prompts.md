# Lava Tube Surveyor "Ariadne" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "LT-1 Ariadne," a tethered lava-tube survey drone,
drawn as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan
linework, faint drafting grid. Main view is a vertical cross-section through the ground: a
hatched surface line at top labeled "surface — radiation, impacts, temp swings," with
arrows striking it labeled "cosmic rays · solar particles · micrometeorites." Beneath the
surface is a thick banded layer labeled "tens of metres of rock = free shielding." A gap in
the surface forms a "skylight (collapse pit)" with sloping broken walls leading down into a
large hollow cavern labeled "lava tube void — dark since formation · steady temp," with a
scatter of rounded blocks on the floor labeled "breakdown rubble." On the surface beside the
pit sits a boxed unit labeled "anchor + winch + relay," from which a cable descends into the
cavern labeled "tether: load + power + data (radio will not escape a cave)." Suspended
mid-void on the tether is a small rectangular drone labeled "LT-1 surveyor," casting two
triangular lamp cones left and right labeled "lamps," with dashed scan rays fanning out to
the cave roof and floor labeled "3D scan of the void." Inset panel top-right titled "five
questions to answer": "how big? — 3D map: does a settlement fit; how safe? — radiation inside
vs surface; how stable? — roof, walls, collapse risk; how steady? — temperature over time;
how usable? — floor, and a way in and out," captioned "is this a shelter we can use — or a
trap we should avoid?" Magnified detail circle showing a small rounded habitat module sitting
on the cave floor beneath a banded rock roof labeled "rock roof = shield," captioned "light
habitat — supplies only air, no burying required," labeled "detail A — why it's worth
looking." A bordered panel reads "! NOTHING ESCAPES A CAVE: not light · not radio · not a
drone that sticks; severed tether = mission over · rims crumble; some surveyors will be lost.
Go anyway." Numbered callout circles, a parts list panel, and a title block reading "LT-1
ARIADNE — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic projection,
no shading, no photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: drone descending into a lunar skylight)

```
Photorealistic render of a compact survey drone descending on a taut tether into a lunar
collapse pit: a rugged instrument package about the size of a suitcase, bristling with a
scanning lidar head, stereo cameras, and bright white floodlights, hanging from a thin
armoured cable that runs up out of frame toward a sunlit rim far above. The drone's lamps
throw hard cones of light across a curving wall of dark basalt, catching the rough texture of
ancient rock and picking out a shadowed rubble-strewn floor far below. Everything outside the
beams falls into total blackness. Fine engineering detail: a compact winch collar, thermal
blanketing, dust covers, a small radiator, cabling, and a stencilled label "LAVA TUBE SURVEY
01." A shaft of harsh sunlight enters through the pit mouth overhead, illuminating hanging
dust motes and a slice of the pit wall, contrasting with the darkness of the cave. Shot at a
dramatic angle looking slightly down past the drone into the void, hyper-detailed, aerospace
documentary photography style, realistic and grounded.
```
