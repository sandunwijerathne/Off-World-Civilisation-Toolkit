# Lunar Mass Driver "Trebuchet" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "LM-1 Trebuchet," a lunar electromagnetic mass
driver, drawn as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue background, fine white
and cyan linework, faint drafting grid. Main view is a side profile: a long straight
accelerator track rising gently from left to right across a hatched "lunar surface (1/6 g,
no air)." Along the track are many short vertical tick marks labeled "drive coils —
switched in sequence." A small rectangular "bucket + payload" sits at the low left start;
speed labels "slow," "faster," "launch speed" run along the track. At the upper-right end
is a "release" point, from which a curved arrow arcs up and off to a small package labeled
"cargo coasts to orbit." A dashed curved arrow returns along the track labeled "bucket
braked + returned (reused)." At lower left, a box labeled "solar / surface power" with a
lightning symbol feeds "pulsed electricity → coils." Inset panel top-right titled "no
propellant rides along" showing a tall rocket that is mostly fuel with a tiny payload
labeled "rocket: mostly fuel ✗" beside a small lone payload block labeled "driver: all
payload ✓," captioned "energy stays on the ground, as electricity." Magnified detail
circle showing a rail with drive coils and a bucket, an arrow "coils pull bucket forward,"
captioned "same as maglev / aircraft catapult" and "detail A — drive coils." A bordered
panel reads "! WHAT IT IS — AND ISN'T — FOR: durable bulk cargo only, not people (hard g);
launches outward only, needs a catcher in orbit; infrastructure for a Moon that already
produces." Numbered callout circles, a parts list panel, and a title block reading "LM-1
TREBUCHET — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic
projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: mass driver on the lunar surface)

```
Photorealistic render of a lunar electromagnetic mass driver on the Moon's surface: a
long, low, straight rail structure of segmented drive-coil modules stretching toward the
horizon along a gentle rise across grey cratered regolith, its rails glinting in hard
sunlight. A compact launch bucket carrying a block of processed metal sits partway along
the track. At the far elevated end, the track releases into open space. Fine engineering
detail: coil housings and cabling running along the track, support pylons anchored into
the regolith, a low control building and a field of solar panels at the near end, dust
kicked up around the base, hazard markings and a label "MASS DRIVER 01." The black sky is
star-dusted with the blue-white crescent of Earth low over the horizon; the lighting is
harsh, high-contrast, with long sharp shadows across the dusty ground. Shot at a low
three-quarter angle emphasising the length of the track, hyper-detailed, aerospace
documentary photography style, realistic and grounded.
```
