# The Ergosphere Siphon

*Category: Black Holes & Extreme Physics · Off-World Civilisation Toolkit*

A spinning black hole is a flywheel with the mass of a star, and general relativity says the rotational energy is genuinely extractable. Inside the ergosphere — the region where space itself is dragged around faster than light can resist — the Penrose process allows a clean heist: drop mass in on the right trajectory, split it, let one half fall past the horizon on a negative-energy orbit, and the other half exits carrying more energy than the whole package brought in. The Siphon is a ring station that industrialises this: an automated mass-driver loop feeding waste mass inward and harvesting the amplified kinetic energy of what returns, converting a black hole into the most durable power plant physically possible.

The mathematics has been settled since Penrose published in 1969; magnetic variants of the same extraction likely power real quasar jets we observe today. Everything else — the station, the materials, the trip there — is engineering at a scale centuries beyond us.

**Status: the far horizon of the catalogue, and the most beautiful physics in it.**
