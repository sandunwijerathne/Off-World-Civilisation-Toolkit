## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "ES-1 Tartarus," a Penrose-process power
station orbiting a spinning black hole, drawn as a vintage cyanotype schematic:
deep prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan linework, faint drafting grid.
Center: a solid black disc labeled "event horizon," surrounded by a dashed oblate
ellipse labeled "ergosphere / static limit," with curved cyan frame-dragging swirl
arrows around it and a small spin-axis arrow labeled "spin J" at the top. A large
solid ellipse drawn around the whole thing represents the ring station's orbit,
with small rectangular station-node modules spaced along it labeled "ring station."
A small circle labeled "waste mass in" follows a dashed trajectory inward to a
bright split point inside the ergosphere; from that point one arrow curves into
the horizon labeled "neg-energy fragment to hole," and another arrow curves back
out to the station labeled "escapes with more energy." Inset panel top-right: a
"Penrose cycle" diagram showing an incoming particle splitting into an outgoing
piece (E greater than E0) and an infalling piece (E less than 0), with notes
"gain drawn from spin," "floor = irreducible mass," "nature's version: quasar
jets." Magnified detail circle showing an energy-budget bar split into irreducible
mass and extractable spin (up to 29%). Numbered callout circles with leader lines,
a parts list panel, and an engineering title block reading "ES-1 TARTARUS —
GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic
projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```
