## Blueprint prompt

Technical engineering blueprint of the "AW-1 Aquarius," an atmospheric water condenser

white and cyan linework, faint drafting grid. Main view is a left-to-right schematic:
arrows labeled "thin Martian air (~1/100 Earth), trace water vapour" flow into a circular
"air mover" fan labeled "draws large volume," which pushes air into a rectangular "cold
trap (below frost point)" containing several vertical cold plates dotted with small frost
marks, labeled "frost deposits on cold plates." Rays point upward from the trap labeled

"drier air out." A downward arrow labeled "warm → melt" leads to a storage vessel with

captioned "the condenser doesn't out-produce the mines — it runs where the mines are not.
A well dug anywhere." Magnified detail circle showing a cold surface with water-vapour

parts list panel, and a title block reading "AW-1 AQUARIUS — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT."
Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic projection, no shading, no photorealism.
