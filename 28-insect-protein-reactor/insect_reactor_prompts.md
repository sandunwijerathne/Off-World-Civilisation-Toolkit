## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "IP-1 Gregor," a waste-to-protein insect

background, fine white and cyan linework, faint drafting grid. Main view is a
left-to-right process flow: a bin labeled "crop waste + scraps (stems, leaves,
residue)" with an arrow labeled "feed prep" leading into a large rounded reactor
labeled "rearing reactor (stacked trays)," drawn as a box containing four horizontal
trays each dotted with tiny curved larvae marks. From the reactor, an arrow labeled
"harvest" leads to a box labeled "process → protein flour (dried, milled, blended)"

"frass (insect droppings)" with an arrow "→ fertilises crops" and a note "(which feed
the insects)," closing the loop. Inset panel top-right titled "you cannot bring a cow":
a simple cow icon labeled "tonnes · years · huge feed + water ✗" beside small larvae

little water/space; FAO-endorsed sustainable protein." Magnified detail circle showing

A — rearing tray." A bordered panel reads "! THE HARDEST PROBLEM IS HUMAN: will the crew

reading "IP-1 GREGOR — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat
orthographic projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```
