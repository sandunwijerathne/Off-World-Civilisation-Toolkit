# The Dazzler Constellation

*Category: Weaponry (Conceptual) · Off-World Civilisation Toolkit*

The most dangerous moment in any orbital standoff is the one where a nation must choose between doing nothing and destroying something. Destruction in orbit is uniquely self-harming — every kill creates a debris cloud that outlives the war. The Dazzler is the rung between: a constellation of laser satellites that blind hostile sensors *temporarily*, saturating optical and infrared detectors with noise without damaging a single component. The target satellite goes dark for minutes or hours, its mission denied, and when the beam lifts, it works again. A weapon whose effects are fully reversible.

Ground-based laser dazzling of satellites is already documented practice among major powers; moving the emitter to orbit shortens ranges and widens geometry, turning a crude tool into a precise one. Strategists call this a de-escalation weapon: it gives leaders a proportionate move that doesn't shatter the commons.

**Status: buildable now; included because the alternative weapons are worse.**
