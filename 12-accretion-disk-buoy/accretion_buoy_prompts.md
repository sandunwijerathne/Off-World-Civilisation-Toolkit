# Accretion-Disk Weather Buoy "Halcyon" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "AB-1 Halcyon," a swarm of radiation-
hardened probes studying a black hole's accretion disk, drawn as a vintage
cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan linework,
faint drafting grid. Center: a small solid black disc (the black hole) surrounded
by a set of concentric flattened ellipses representing an edge-on accretion disk,
with a cyan curved arrow labeled "hot plasma, MRI turbulence" and thin double-line
relativistic jets projecting up and down from the poles labeled "jet." Around the
disk, large dashed elliptical orbits carry several small rectangular buoy icons
labeled "buoy (sacrificial sensor)," dipping close to the disk and swinging out.
To the right, a larger better-shielded relay craft with a dish and panels labeled
"relay craft (safe distance)," with dashed data-relay arrows passing outward from
buoys to relay to "home." Inset panel lower-left: a graph titled "dip-and-retreat
orbit" plotting radiation dose spiking briefly at "peak dose" during the sample
pass and low during "transmit and cool." Magnified detail circle showing a single
hardened buoy: concentric heavy shielding shells around a small core, with a
magnetometer probe boom. Numbered callout circles with leader lines, a parts list
panel, and an engineering title block reading "AB-1 HALCYON — GENERAL
ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic projection, no
shading, no photorealism.
```
