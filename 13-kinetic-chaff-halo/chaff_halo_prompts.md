## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "CH-1 Aegis," a self-organising micro-
satellite defence swarm, drawn as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-
blue background, fine white and cyan linework, faint drafting grid. Center: a
small protected space station drawn as a boxy module with two solar panels,
labeled "asset." Around it, a large dashed circle labeled "resting halo" studded
with many small white dots representing sparse swarm units in formation. From the
upper right, a single object with a bold arrow streaks inward labeled "incoming
8 km/s." On the threat-facing side, a dense cluster of cyan dots is drawn labeled
"swarm thickens here," with small dashed migration arrows showing units moving
from the halo toward the intercept point, and a small shatter-spray of lines at
the impact. Inset panel top-right: a five-step "intercept sequence" diagram —
detect, predict, thicken, absorb (Whipple shatter), heal — with small icons and
arrows. Magnified detail circle of a single fist-sized unit showing micro-
thrusters at the corners, a sensor eye, a comms antenna, and a processor grid,
labeled "CPU + bumper body." Numbered callout circles with leader lines, a parts
list panel, and an engineering title block reading "CH-1 AEGIS — GENERAL
ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic projection, no
shading, no photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: small debris-defence swarm around a station)

```
Photorealistic render of a near-future space station in low Earth orbit surrounded
by a loose, sparse cloud of dozens of small cube-shaped micro-satellites, each
about the size of a fist, matte grey with tiny gold thruster nozzles, small
antennas, and blinking status LEDs, holding formation in a rough spherical halo
around the station. The station is a realistic modular structure with solar arrays
and radiators. A few of the micro-satellites on one side are clustered slightly
denser, catching sunlight, as if responding to something. Earth's blue curvature
and cloud tops fill the background below, with the terminator line and city lights
on the night side. Fine engineering detail on the micro-sats: cold-gas thruster
ports, sensor lenses, a printed serial on the station "GUARDIAN-1," subtle motion
blur on a couple of repositioning units. Shot from a three-quarter angle by an
inspection camera, hard sunlight from the left, deep black space with stars,
hyper-detailed, aerospace documentary photography style.
```
