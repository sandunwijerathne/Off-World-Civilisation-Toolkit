# The Kinetic Chaff Halo

*Category: Defense Systems · Off-World Civilisation Toolkit*

Armour that thinks. The Chaff Halo is a shield made of thousands of coordinated micro-satellites orbiting a station like a school of fish, each one a fist-sized unit with cold-gas thrusters, a radar cross-section it can change, and a shared tactical brain. When debris or a projectile approaches, the swarm computes an intercept lattice in milliseconds and densifies exactly where the threat will pass — soaking the impact with expendable units whose replacements are already drifting into formation. Against sensors, the same swarm scatters into a confusing, shifting cloud of false targets.

Every ingredient exists: mega-constellations already fly thousands of coordinated satellites, swarm-control algorithms are a mature research field, and whipple shielding — sacrificial standoff layers — has protected spacecraft for decades. The Halo simply makes the shield active, distributed, and self-healing.

**Status: buildable this decade in crude form; treaty questions are the real barrier.**
