Which mode per shot

AFrames to Video. First frame = blueprint PNG, last frame = render PNG. The linework ignites into the machine.
BIngredients to Video. Render as Ingredient 1, labelled with the machine name. Blueprint as Ingredient 2 holds detail accuracy.
CSame ingredients, or extend the winning B clip in Scenebuilder — that matches better than a fresh prompt.

Label the ingredient with the exact machine name in the prompt. A mismatch is the commonest cause of Flow inventing different hardware.

Reference contract

Every B and C prompt opens with it. Do not delete it — it is what stops Veo redesigning your hardware between shots.

Negative prompt

Paste into Flow's negative field. Blueprint SVGs are 1400 × 1000 — export at 2800 × 2000 and pad onto a 1080 × 1920 prussian-blue canvas for Shot A's first frame.
