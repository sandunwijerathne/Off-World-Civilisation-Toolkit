# Regolith Rail Grader "Appius" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "RG-1 Appius," a regolith road-sintering crawler,
drawn as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan
linework, faint drafting grid. Main view is a side elevation of a low, heavy tracked
crawler moving right to left along a ground line. Ahead of it (right) the ground is drawn
as scattered loose grain dots labeled "loose regolith ahead — sharp, clinging, dusty";
behind it (left) the ground is a solid hatched band labeled "fused road — bound, no dust."
At the machine's leading edge is a triangular "grade blade — levels, clears rocks." On top
of the crawler is a large parabolic "solar concentrator (free heat)" with converging sun
rays and a focused beam arrow pointing down into the ground. Inside the body is a boxed
"microwave head — works at night" with wave symbols. Under the machine a highlighted band
labeled "sinter zone — grains soften + bond," with a dashed arrow trailing behind labeled
"cools to hard crust." Inset panel top-right titled "why bind the ground": a small rover on
loose ground throwing curved dust trails labeled "loose ✗ — dust flung far (no air to stop
it)," and below it the same rover on a solid fused strip labeled "fused ✓ clean," captioned
"traffic = the biggest dust source." Magnified detail circle showing loose separate grains
on the left, a "heat" arrow, and bonded grains with small necks joining them on the right,
captioned "necks form → bonded" and "sintered, not melted · no binder," labeled "detail A —
sintering." A bordered panel reads "! SLOW AND POWER-HUNGRY: energy per unit area is the
limit — roads are big; crust is ceramic: strong in compression, cracks in tension; but every
strip fused is fused forever." Numbered callout circles, a parts list panel, and a title
block reading "RG-1 APPIUS — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat
orthographic projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: pad-making sintering crawler on the Moon)

```
Photorealistic render of a slow tracked road-sintering machine working on the lunar surface:
a low, boxy, heavily built crawler on wide dusty tracks, with a curved polished solar
concentrator mirror mounted above it focusing a brilliant point of light onto the ground just
beneath its belly. The regolith directly under the focus glows a fierce orange-white; behind
the machine stretches a finished strip of dark, smooth, glassy fused pavement, visibly
distinct from the loose grey powdery regolith ahead of it. Fine engineering detail: a grading
blade at the front, thermal shielding and radiator fins, dust-sealed track bogies caked in
grey dust, cabling, hazard striping and a label "SINTER GRADER 01," a small camera mast. The
setting is a level plain with a habitat module and lander in the far distance, black
star-dusted sky, harsh low sunlight casting long sharp shadows. The glow of the sinter point
lights the underside of the machine. Shot at a low three-quarter angle emphasising the
contrast between fused road and loose dust, hyper-detailed, industrial documentary
photography style, realistic and grounded.
```
