# The Asteroid Herder

*Category: Defense Systems · Off-World Civilisation Toolkit*

Planetary defense is the one weapon system every nation wants its rivals to have. The Herder is a standing fleet of gravity tractors: spacecraft that park beside a hazardous asteroid and simply hover there for years, letting their own tiny gravitational pull — augmented by ion thrusters angled to avoid pushing on the rock directly — tow the asteroid's orbit by millimetres per second. Given a decade of warning, millimetres per second is the difference between an impact and a miss measured in Earth-radii.

The physics needs no defense: NASA's DART mission slammed into Dimorphos in 2022 and measurably changed its orbit, proving humanity can move asteroids; the gravity tractor is the gentle sibling for cases where fragments are unacceptable. The Herder fleet's real innovation is being *pre-deployed* — stationed at asteroid-belt waypoints before a threat is found, because the tractor technique spends years, and warning time is the one thing you cannot manufacture.

**Status: every component flight-proven; needs only the decision to stand the fleet up.**
