## Most-buildable real product prompt (a gravity tractor station-keeping beside an asteroid)

```
Photorealistic render of a large gravity-tractor spacecraft hovering a short
distance from a rugged grey near-Earth asteroid in deep space. The spacecraft is a
substantial deep-space probe with a boxy central body wrapped in gold and black
thermal insulation, two enormous deep-blue solar array wings, and glowing blue ion
thrusters mounted at angles so their faint blue exhaust plumes point outward, away
from the asteroid, so as not to push on it. The asteroid is a dark, heavily
cratered, irregular rocky body filling the left of the frame, lit hard from one
side. The spacecraft holds station just off its surface, connected to it by
nothing but proximity. Background: pure black space, dense sharp stars, a distant
small Sun. Fine engineering detail: high-gain dish antenna aimed away, xenon
propellant tanks, navigation cameras, radiators, a printed label "GRAVITY TRACTOR
- PDCO." Shot from a three-quarter angle, hard raking sunlight, hyper-detailed,
aerospace documentary photography style.
```
