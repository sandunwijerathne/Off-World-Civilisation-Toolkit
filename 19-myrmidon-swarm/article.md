# The Myrmidon Swarm

*Category: AI Robots · Off-World Civilisation Toolkit*

A ship's hull should heal like skin. The Myrmidons are insect-scale repair robots — thousands per vessel — that live in the hull's service channels, patrolling for micrometeoroid punctures, fatigue cracks, and corrosion. Each unit carries patch material, a micro-welder, and adhesion feet for any surface orientation. Their defining feature is a shared mind in the practical sense: federated learning across the swarm means that when one Myrmidon fumbles through repairing a novel fracture geometry, the learned solution propagates to every unit by the next synchronisation cycle. One learns; a million know.

Microrobotics already produces insect-scale walkers and climbers in university labs; federated learning is deployed commercially at planetary scale in phones. The fusion — durable micro-machines with collective skill acquisition, operating for years in vacuum — is an integration challenge with no physics barrier at all.

**Status: terrestrial industrial versions (pipelines, refineries) will arrive first and fund the space variant.**
