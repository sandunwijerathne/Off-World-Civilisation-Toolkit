# Myrmidon Swarm "MY-1" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "MY-1 Myrmidon," an insect-scale hull-repair
robot swarm, drawn as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue background,
fine white and cyan linework, faint drafting grid. Main view: a horizontal
cross-section of a spacecraft hull drawn as two parallel lines with hatching below
and vertical panel seams, labeled "spacecraft hull (section)." Crawling along the
top surface are several small insect-like robots drawn as simple oval bodies with
four thin legs. An arrow comes in from above labeled "micrometeoroid / debris
impact," striking the hull and leaving a small crater with a branching crack
labeled "micro-crack." Three robots cluster around the damage labeled "work party
recruited to damage," with dashed arcs from distant robots labeled "local recruit
signal." At the right end sits a small "dock" charging station. Inset panel
top-right titled "one learns, all know": three robot icons on the left labeled
"local experience" with arrows feeding into a central box labeled "shared model,"
and a dashed arrow curving back out labeled "back to all," captioned "model
updates, not raw data (low bandwidth)." Magnified detail circle of a single robot
showing an oval body, four legs ending in round "dry-adhesion grip pads," a sensor
eye, a comms antenna, a solar strip on its back, and a small repair applicator.
A small dimension line labeled "insect scale." Numbered callout circles with
leader lines, a parts list panel, and an engineering title block reading "MY-1
MYRMIDON — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic
projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: hull-inspection crawler on a station exterior)

```
Photorealistic macro render of a small robotic crawler about the size of a human
hand clinging to the exterior hull panel of a space station in low Earth orbit.
The robot has a low, flat matte-white body with a visible small solar strip, a
tiny camera lens on a short stalk, a stub antenna, and six delicate articulated
legs ending in wide textured grip pads pressed against the white multi-layer
insulation and metal panel surface. Fine detail: hull panel seams, rivets, a faint
micrometeoroid pit near the robot, scuffing and slight discoloration on the
insulation, a printed serial label "HULL INSPECTOR 07," a status LED. In the
background, out-of-focus station structure, a handrail, and the blue curve of
Earth with cloud tops. Shot as an extreme close-up with shallow depth of field,
hard raking sunlight from one side casting a sharp shadow of the robot on the
hull, deep black space above, hyper-detailed, aerospace documentary photography
style, realistic and grounded.
```
