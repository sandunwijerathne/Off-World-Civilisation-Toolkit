# The Planetary Power Loop

*Category: Off-World Civilisation Tools · Off-World Civilisation Toolkit*

A colony's most fragile organ is its power supply — dust storms starve solar panels, and night always comes. The Power Loop removes the atmosphere from the equation: constellations of orbital collectors gather raw sunlight around the clock, convert it to microwaves, and beam it to rectenna farms — hectares of simple wire mesh — on the settlement's surface. The beam is diffuse enough to be harmless to anything walking through it, the ground hardware is nearly maintenance-free, and power keeps flowing through storm, night, and winter.

Space-based solar power crossed from theory to demonstration when Caltech's SSPD-1 beamed detectable power from orbit in 2023, and both JAXA and ESA run active programs. Ironically, the case is stronger off-Earth than on it: launch costs from a colony's shallow gravity well are lower, and the alternative grids are worse.

**Status: the first megawatt-class loop could plausibly power a lunar base by mid-century.**
