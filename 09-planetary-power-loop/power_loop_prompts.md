Technical engineering blueprint of the "PL-1 Helios Ring," an orbital solar-power

drafting grid. The scene is a large system schematic: a stylized sun with rays at

flat orthographic projection, no shading, no photorealism.

Photorealistic split-scene render of a near-future space-based solar power
