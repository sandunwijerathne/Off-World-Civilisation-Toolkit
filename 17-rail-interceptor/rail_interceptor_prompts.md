# Hypervelocity Rail Interceptor "RI-1" — Image Generation Prompts

> **Note on this concept's prompts.** Unlike the other entries in this catalogue,
> this study deliberately has **no "buildable product" prompt**. This is a weapon
> concept, presented as a caution rather than a proposal, so both prompts below
> are illustrative and conceptual only — a schematic and a museum-style
> exhibition piece. Neither depicts buildable engineering.

## Blueprint prompt (principle schematic only)

```
Technical blueprint-style schematic of a conceptual orbital kinetic point-defence
system, drawn as a vintage cyanotype diagram: deep prussian-blue background, fine
white and cyan linework, faint drafting grid. Deliberately abstract and
block-level, clearly labeled "PRINCIPLE SCHEMATIC ONLY — NOT AN ENGINEERING
DRAWING." Left to right: a simple rectangular block labeled "solar power," a flow
arrow to a plain rectangle labeled "energy store (block-level only)," a flow arrow
to two long parallel horizontal lines representing an electromagnetic accelerator
shown purely schematically, with a small plain rectangle between them labeled
"inert mass." A dashed arrow leaves the accelerator to the right labeled "high
closing speed," with the caption "no warhead — motion is the energy." Upper right:
a small circle labeled "incoming threat" on an arrow path, meeting the projectile
path at a small starburst labeled "kinetic intercept." Below the accelerator,
angled lines labeled "waste heat radiators." A prominent bordered panel on the
right reads "! GOVERNANCE, NOT ENGINEERING" with bullet points: defensive and
offensive forms nearly identical; role set by intent, not design; kinetic hits
create long-lived debris; destabilising — belongs to arms control; and the line
"presented as a caution, not a blueprint." Numbered callouts, a principle index
panel, and a title block reading "RI-1 — PRINCIPLE SCHEMATIC (CONCEPTUAL)" with
the field "framing: govern, don't build." Monospaced drafting typography, flat
orthographic projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```

## Illustrative concept-art prompt (museum exhibit — not a buildable product)

```
Photorealistic render of a museum exhibition hall in a future space-policy
institute, presenting an educational display about orbital weapons and why they
were restricted. At the centre, a stylised, deliberately simplified scale model of
a conceptual orbital interceptor sits under glass on a plinth — an abstract,
sculptural form with a long slender rail structure and solar panels, matte grey
and gold, clearly a model rather than working hardware. Behind it, a large
backlit wall panel shows a cyanotype-style schematic and the heading "KINETIC
WEAPONS IN ORBIT: THE CASE FOR RESTRAINT," alongside an infographic of an
expanding debris cloud spreading around Earth. Museum visitors stand reading the
panels. Warm gallery spotlights, polished concrete floor, deep shadows,
architectural photography style, shallow depth of field on the model, thoughtful
and sombre mood. NOTE: an illustrative exhibition piece about arms control — not a
buildable product render.
```
