# The Hypervelocity Rail Interceptor

*Category: Weaponry (Conceptual) · Off-World Civilisation Toolkit*

In orbit, speed is the explosive. A slug arriving at 10 km/s carries more energy per kilogram than TNT, so an orbital defense railgun needs no warhead at all — just electromagnetic rails, a capacitor bank charged patiently from solar panels, and inert tungsten darts. The Interceptor concept is a station-defense turret: radar hands it a track on an incoming kill vehicle or large debris object, the capacitors dump megajoules in milliseconds, and the dart meets the threat at closing speeds that reduce both to vapour and gravel.

The US Navy built and test-fired working railguns for years before shelving the program — the physics is settled, and orbit actually cures the technology's earthly diseases: no atmosphere to slow the projectile, no gravity droop over range, and vacuum for the rails. Presented here as a defensive point-intercept system; the same machine pointed downward is a treaty violation, which is precisely why the concept matters to think about *before* someone builds it.

**Status: physics proven; deployment is a political event, not a technical one.**
