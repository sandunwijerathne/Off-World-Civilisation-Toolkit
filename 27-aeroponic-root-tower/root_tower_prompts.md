# Aeroponic Root Tower "Antaeus" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "AR-1 Antaeus," an aeroponic root tower, drawn
as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan
linework, faint drafting grid. Main view is a vertical cutaway of a growing tower: a
tall rounded rectangle with a darker interior chamber. Around its outer surface, several
small plants are set into ports so their leafy tops fan outward into the light while
their fine roots pass through the wall and hang down inside the dark chamber. Inside,
small mist nozzles spray fine cones of droplets over the hanging roots, labeled "mist
nozzles." Grow-light rays come in from both sides labeled "grow light." At the base, a
sloped sump labeled "runoff collected"; a curved pipe carries it to a "reservoir + pump
(nutrient mix)," and a pressurised feed pipe returns to the nozzles, forming a loop
labeled "water used many times." Labels read "leaves in light" and "roots in dark misted
chamber." Inset panel top-right titled "why misting beats flooding" showing a flooded
root box labeled "flooded: drowns ✗" beside a misted root box with tiny droplets labeled
"misted: breathes + drinks ✓," captioned "roots need water + oxygen, which compete; mist
gives both and suits microgravity (no pools)." A bordered panel reads "! MINUTES FROM
DROUGHT: no soil = no moisture buffer; pump fails / nozzle clogs / power drops → bare
roots dry out fast → crop lost; demands redundant pumps, vigilant monitoring, balanced
nutrient mix." Numbered callout circles, a parts list panel, and a title block reading
"AR-1 ANTAEUS — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic
projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: aeroponic salad tower in a habitat)

```
Photorealistic render of a vertical aeroponic growing tower inside a space habitat: a
sleek white cylindrical column about two metres tall, studded around its surface with
lush green lettuce, herbs, and leafy salad plants growing out of small ports, their tops
bright under integrated pink-white LED grow-lights. A cutaway window in the column reveals
bare pale roots hanging inside a dark chamber, glistening with a fine mist. Fine detail:
neat plumbing and a nutrient reservoir at the base with a small pump and a control panel
reading "AEROPONIC TOWER 01," condensation and tiny droplets on the interior, a harvest
basket of picked greens beside it, Velcro tool patches, and the technical interior of the
habitat softly out of focus behind. The grow-lights cast a healthy pink-green glow on
nearby surfaces. Shot as a crisp three-quarter product photograph, clean and vibrant,
hyper-detailed, aerospace documentary photography style, realistic and grounded.
```
