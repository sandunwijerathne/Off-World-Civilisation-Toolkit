## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "CB-1 Cryobot," a nuclear-heated melting
probe for Europa, drawn as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue
background, fine white and cyan linework, faint drafting grid. Main view is a
vertical cross-section: a horizontal line at the top labeled "Europa surface,"
with a small four-legged lander and dish antenna sitting on it labeled "surface
station," and a dashed signal arrow going up and off-frame. Below the surface, a
tall block of ice shell with faint diagonal texture hatching, labeled "ice shell,
km-scale, thickness uncertain," containing two dashed ellipses labeled "brine
pocket" and "void." A narrow vertical channel runs down from the lander, drawn
with dashed walls and small cross-ticks labeled "channel refreezes behind," with
three tiny rectangles embedded in it labeled "frozen-in relays." At the bottom of
the channel sits the cryobot: a slender vertical cylinder with a rounded, heated
nose, with small heat marks and a meltwater pool beneath it, labeled "heated nose
melts ice." Below the ice, a horizontal line opens into a region drawn with wavy
cyan lines labeled "global salt-water ocean," captioned "breakthrough — swims as
submersible." A vertical dimension line on the left reads "shell thickness —
months of descent." Inset panel top-right titled "why it must decide alone":
Earth, a long dashed arrow labeled "~1 hr each way," Europa, a thick bar labeled
"ice blocks radio entirely," and a small probe icon beyond it, with the caption
"autonomy is not a convenience — it is the payload." Magnified detail circle
showing the probe's internal stack: tether dispenser, autonomy and instruments,
sampling sensors, nuclear heat source, melt nose. A bordered panel reads "!
PLANETARY PROTECTION: ocean may already hold life; contact would be irreversible;
extreme sterilisation required; if not clean enough — do not fly." Numbered callout
circles, a parts list panel, and a title block reading "CB-1 CRYOBOT — GENERAL
ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic projection, no
shading, no photorealism.
```
