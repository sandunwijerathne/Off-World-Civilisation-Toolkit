# Hopper Freighter "Grasshopper" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "HF-1 Grasshopper," a ballistic cargo hopper, drawn
as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan
linework, faint drafting grid. Main view is a flight profile across a wide landscape
section: a jagged ground line of scalloped crater rims and rubble labeled "craters · rilles
· boulder fields — impassable to rovers," with a flat hatched "sintered pad (base)" at the
left end and another marked "remote site" at the right. A long dashed parabolic arc spans
between them labeled "ballistic arc — coast (no air, no weather)." Three small vehicle
figures appear along the path: at left, upright with a flame plume labeled "1 · launch
burn"; at the apex, engine off labeled "2 · coast (engine off)"; at right, flipped with a
retro plume labeled "3 · landing burn (no air to brake on)." A dimension line beneath reads
"hopper: under an hour · rover: months, if at all." Magnified detail circle at right showing
the vehicle in elevation: a "cargo deck (no crew)" on top, a "propellant" tank body, a bell
engine labeled "throttleable, restartable," and four splayed landing legs, captioned "no
wings · no heat shield · no streamlining," labeled "detail A — vehicle (stripped to
essentials)." Inset panel top-right titled "three modes, three jobs": "rail / road — cheap,
paved network only; mass driver — cheapest, off-world only; hopper — costly, but reaches
anywhere," captioned "roads carry the tonnage. The hopper carries the reach." A bordered
panel reads "! IT PAYS PROPELLANT TWICE: once to launch, once to land — no air to brake on;
unaffordable without local propellant production; landing blasts debris → wants sintered
pads; no rescue: an engine that won't restart loses all." Numbered callout circles, a parts
list panel, and a title block reading "HF-1 GRASSHOPPER — GENERAL ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced
drafting typography, flat orthographic projection, no shading, no photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: hopper landing at a remote lunar site)

```
Photorealistic render of a small uncrewed cargo hopper touching down on the lunar surface: a
squat utilitarian lander, a cluster of spherical propellant tanks wrapped in gold and white
thermal blanketing on an exposed metal frame, a single throttled engine firing a pale
translucent plume beneath it, four splayed landing legs with wide footpads just above a
sintered dark landing pad. The engine plume kicks a low fan of grey dust outward across the
pad. Strapped to its upper deck is a secured cargo container stencilled "CARGO." Fine
engineering detail: thrust structure, plumbing runs, RCS thruster clusters, a navigation
camera and lidar head, hazard striping, and a label "HOPPER 01." Setting: a remote lunar
plain with a distant crater rim on the horizon, a small survey outpost in the mid-distance,
black star-dusted sky, harsh low sunlight throwing long sharp shadows. Shot at a low
three-quarter angle capturing the moment before touchdown, hyper-detailed, aerospace
documentary photography style, realistic and grounded.
```
