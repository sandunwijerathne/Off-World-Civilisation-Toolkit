# Solar Concentrator Kiln "Archimedes" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "SC-1 Archimedes," a lunar solar concentrator kiln,
drawn as a vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan
linework, faint drafting grid. Main view is a side elevation: parallel arrows descend from
the top labeled "full solar flux — no air to absorb, scatter, or cloud it," striking a row of
four tilted flat mirrors on short posts along a hatched ground line, labeled "heliostat field
— only the mirrors track the sun." From each mirror a straight reflected beam runs up and
right, all four converging on a single small boxed receiver mounted atop a slender tower,
labeled "fixed receiver kiln," with a bright point at its centre and a note "furnace stays
still (feed + plumbing don't move)." Text along the converging beams reads "all light → one
small focus." An arrow into the receiver is labeled "feed in" and an arrow out is labeled
"processed out." A bordered box at lower left reads "no fuel · no boiler · no turbine;
sunlight → heat, directly; no electrical conversion loss." Inset panel top-right titled "one
field, most of the volume's heat" listing "sinter regolith → dome · rail grader; melt basalt →
fibre spinnery; boil metals → vapour refinery; melt feedstock → orbital foundry; bake
volatiles → water + gas extraction," captioned "the shared thermal utility of an industrial
site." Magnified detail circle showing a wide horizontal line labeled "large collecting area"
with five arrows converging down onto a single small bright dot labeled "small focal area,"
captioned "ratio = concentration = temperature" and "target loses heat only by radiating,"
labeled "detail A — concentration." A bordered panel reads "! THE SUN SETS, AND THE DUST
STAYS: lunar night ≈ 14 Earth days — kiln makes nothing; mirrors work only while clean — dust
is permanent work; pair with electric heat: mirrors by day, power for precision." Numbered
callout circles, a parts list panel, and a title block reading "SC-1 ARCHIMEDES — GENERAL
ARRANGEMENT." Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic projection, no shading, no
photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: tracking concentrator sintering regolith)

```
Photorealistic render of a solar concentrator kiln operating on the lunar surface: a polished
parabolic dish mirror roughly three metres across, mounted on a motorised two-axis tracking
frame anchored into the grey regolith, angled toward a low brilliant sun. Its focused beam
converges onto a small ceramic receiver head just above the ground, where the regolith glows a
searing orange-white and a finished strip of dark glassy sintered pavement extends behind. Fine
engineering detail: faceted mirror segments with fine seams, actuator motors and cabling on the
tracking frame, a dust-shrouded base, a thermal-shielded receiver arm, a small control box with
a sun sensor, hazard striping and a stencilled label "SOLAR KILN 01." Grey dust dulls the lower
edges of the mirror. Setting: a level lunar plain with a habitat module and equipment in the
far distance, black star-dusted sky, harsh sunlight and long knife-edged shadows, the focal
point casting an intense warm glow onto the underside of the receiver. Shot at a low
three-quarter angle emphasising the beam converging to its point, hyper-detailed, industrial
documentary photography style, realistic and grounded.
```
