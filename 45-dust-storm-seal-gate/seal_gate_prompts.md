# Dust-Storm Seal Gate "Janus" — Image Generation Prompts

## Blueprint prompt

```
Technical engineering blueprint of the "SG-1 Janus," a graded dust-boundary airlock, drawn as a
vintage cyanotype schematic: deep prussian-blue background, fine white and cyan linework, faint
drafting grid. Main view is a horizontal section captioned "dirtiest at left, cleanest at right —
nothing travels backwards." At the far left, scattered dots represent dust and a hatched ground
line labeled "outside." Three rectangular chambers run left to right, separated by bold vertical
door lines each labeled "door." Zone 1 is labeled "gross removal" and contains small arrows
labeled "gas jet (Mars only)," a wavy line labeled "agitation," and loose dust dots, captioned
"sheds the coarse fraction." Zone 2 is labeled "electrodynamic sweep" and contains a floor line
with a row of short vertical electrode ticks beneath it labeled "electrodes beneath a dielectric
surface," leftward arrows above it labeled "travelling wave marches grains outward," a small
horseshoe magnet labeled "magnetic capture — dust is iron-bearing," and a note "no brushes · no
water · no moving parts." Zone 3 is labeled "pressure lock," captioned "the only part a
terrestrial engineer would call an airlock." To the right of the last door sits a rounded
rectangle labeled "habitat," captioned "clean — and staying that way is a budget." Beneath all
three zones, a long row of arrows points leftward (outward) labeled "directional airflow,
drainage and slope all run outward — dust goes home, not deeper." Inset panel titled "every
mechanism has a different blind spot" listing agitation/brushing, gas jet, electrodynamic sweep,
magnetic capture and directional airflow against their weaknesses, captioned "the residue of one
is the target of the next; stacked, it is still asymptotic — never zero." Magnified detail circle
showing a dielectric surface line with electrodes beneath it, a sine wave labeled "field travels
along the surface," and small dust grains on top with arrows marching them sideways, captioned
"grains charge, lift, and march off," labeled "detail A — travelling wave." Two bordered panels
at the bottom: "! THE BEST GATE IS THE ONE NOBODY WALKS THROUGH — a suit that docks to the
outside of the hull and is entered from within never brings its dusty exterior indoors at all,"
and "! IT IS A BUDGET, NOT A BARRIER — some dust gets through every single cycle; the defence is
that ingress stays below internal removal." Numbered callout circles, a parts list panel, and a
title block. Monospaced drafting typography, flat orthographic projection, no shading, no
photorealism.
```

## Most-buildable real product prompt (Phase 1: electrodynamic decontamination vestibule)

```
Photorealistic render of the interior of a dust decontamination vestibule at a Mars habitat: a
compact chamber with matte white composite walls and a distinctive floor of dark grey dielectric
panels, each panel showing a fine regular pattern of embedded parallel electrode traces just
visible beneath its surface. A dusty ochre-red Mars suit stands on the panels facing an inner
hatch, its lower legs and boots visibly caked in fine rust-coloured dust while the panel surface
beneath is noticeably cleaner, with a faint drift of dust being carried toward a floor-level
grille at the outer end. Fine engineering detail: a high-voltage drive cabinet on the wall with
status lights and a small readout, low-level air return grilles along the outer wall, a magnetic
capture cartridge in a labelled housing, handrails, a sealed inner hatch with a viewport, warning
placards reading "DIRTY SIDE" and "SWEEP ACTIVE — DO NOT ENTER," and a stencilled sign "SEAL GATE
02 — ZONE 2." Through the outer hatch window, a dust-hazed pink-orange Martian sky and rusty
terrain. Cool clinical interior lighting contrasting with the warm dusty light from outside. Shot
at a three-quarter angle emphasising the contrast between the dusty suit and the swept floor,
hyper-detailed, aerospace documentary photography style, realistic and grounded.
```
